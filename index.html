Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register?

No registration is required to start building. The current builder is designed around local-first editing.

Can I change templates later?

Yes. The V2 workspace includes template selection and the gallery can also open the builder with a selected template.

Can I make a CV for a student or fresh graduate?

Yes. The template library is organized by career level, industry and style to support different CV needs.

Where are my working details stored?

The current V2 editor uses browser-based local storage and recovery features rather than requiring an account.
