# M2248–M2327 — CV AI Intelligence Contract

## Purpose
Define a provider-neutral boundary for AI-assisted CV improvements and job tailoring.

## Contract
- Supported tasks: improve, tailor, summarize, suggest.
- AI receives a document snapshot and optional job context/instructions.
- Results remain suggestions requiring human review by default.
- Provider identity and credentials are deliberately outside the application contract.
- The boundary does not auto-apply generated content.

## Validation
The milestone suite covers task validation, job context, review-required results, and provider neutrality.
